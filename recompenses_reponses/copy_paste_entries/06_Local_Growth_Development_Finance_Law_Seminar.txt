Project Overview: Provide a brief description of your project, outlining its main objectives, key activities, and expected impacts. (Word limit:150):
Directed by Tasnim Naggara, the 2026 Finance Law Seminar was convened by JCI Sahline at the Technopole de Monastir in close collaboration with the Délégation de Sahline, the Municipal Council, and regional economic stakeholders. The program featured keynote presentations by Ahmed Ayedi, Regional President of the Order of Chartered Accountants, and Sirine Ben Naceur Bouabid, Senior Representative of the Ministry of Finance. Key activities focused on practical compliance strategies for electronic billing, tax digitization hurdles, and enterprise risk management. Mobilizing extensive corporate sponsorship (including chartered transport and welcome packs by AMAD Groupe), the seminar attracted 100 participants—over half being university business and economics students—enhancing JCI's profile as an intellectual bridge connecting higher education with regional enterprise.
