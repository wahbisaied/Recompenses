Q1: How does your LOM define its annual financial strategy, and how does it align with its strategic objectives and action plan?
JCI Sahline defines its annual financial strategy through a balanced, project-linked budgeting framework approved during our opening General Assembly. Our strategy aligns financial planning directly with our four strategic pillars: community impact, individual member development, business growth, and public health. To ensure fiscal independence, our strategy minimizes reliance on annual membership dues by actively diversifying revenue streams across corporate sponsorships, in-kind resource mobilization, competitive project prize grants, and alumni contributions. Every proposed activity in our annual action plan is assigned an itemized operational budget requiring matching non-cash partnerships before launch, safeguarding the chapter's working capital while ensuring that financial resources directly advance our community objectives.

Q2: What mechanisms and tools do you use to prepare, track, and monitor your budget, manage your expenses, and anticipate financial risks?
Budget preparation begins with historical expenditure analysis and project-level cost estimation led by the Local Treasurer and President. We utilize a standardized digital financial ledger tracking cash flow, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and pledged partner contributions. To manage financial risk, JCI Sahline enforces a dual-approval threshold: all project expenditures require joint written authorization from both the Action Director and the Local Treasurer. Furthermore, peer treasury mentorship serves as an institutional risk-mitigation tool: President Wahbi Saied, having served as Local Treasurer in 2024, provided continuous advisory oversight to 2026 Treasurer Mohamed Akrem Ben Abdesslem, ensuring institutional continuity, prompt bank reconciliations, and adherence to non-profit accounting standards.

Q3: What procedures has your LOM implemented to ensure transparency, traceability, and the proper use of financial resources?
Traceability and transparency are anchored in strict administrative protocols. Every financial transaction—regardless of amount—is backed by a numbered payment voucher, verified invoice, or official supplier receipt. Cash transactions are systematically minimized in favor of verifiable bank transfers. For in-kind contributions (such as venue waivers, event transport, and refreshments), the treasury maintains a dedicated non-cash valuation log documenting the fair market value, contributing partner, and specific project allocation. All original accounting pieces are archived in physical and cloud repositories accessible to the internal auditing committee and legal advisor.

Q4: How do you monitor the organization's financial situation and report financial information to the governing bodies and members?
Financial monitoring operates on a multi-tiered review cycle. The Local Treasurer conducts monthly account reconciliations and presents a comprehensive financial status report at every monthly Board of Directors meeting, reviewing variance between projected and actual outlays. At mid-year and year-end Ordinary General Assemblies, a detailed financial report—including income statements, cash flow analysis, and sponsor fulfillment metrics—is presented to the entire general membership for formal review, debate, and democratic vote of approval, guaranteeing complete organizational accountability.

Q5: What strategy does your LOM implement to identify, prospect, and retain financial sponsors and partners? Specify the approaches used and the results obtained.
Our prospecting strategy relies on needs-based corporate alignment and regional civic networking. Rather than issuing generic donation solicitations, JCI Sahline maps the specific logistical and operational requirements of each initiative against prospective corporate partners' corporate social responsibility (CSR) priorities. For educational programs, we approached private academic institutions (École et Collège AMAD, Lycée Lambda); for business forums, we targeted regional enterprises (Havana Coffee, Technopole de Monastir); and for health actions, we partnered with private sports and medical facilities (Hoist Gym, Hadil Events). This targeted value proposition yielded robust corporate alliances, secured sponsored transport and catering, and unlocked direct competition prize funding.

Q6: How does your LOM create attractive sponsorship packages and value-creating partnerships while ensuring partner retention from year to year?
We create multi-tiered partnership packages (Official Event Partner, Logistical Sponsor, Community Supporter) offering high-impact brand visibility tailored to partner objectives. Sponsors receive prominent logo integration on municipal-scale banners, targeted mentions across our high-performing digital channels (reaching over 400,000 views on Facebook), VIP speaking roles, and formal honor trophies presented before civic dignitaries. We ensure partner retention through rigorous post-event stewardship: every sponsor receives a formal impact report, a certificate of appreciation, and a personalized debrief showcasing audience reach and community outcomes.

Q7: What initiatives has your LOM developed to diversify its funding sources (fundraising events, contributions, partnerships, donations, or other mechanisms)? Present the results achieved.
JCI Sahline successfully diversified its financial architecture across five distinct channels: 1) Corporate cash sponsorship and event underwriting (AMAD Groupe, Lycée Lambda); 2) In-kind logistical resources, including complimentary access to premier amphitheaters (Technopole Monastir) and sports complexes (Hoist Gym); 3) Sponsored services, such as chartered bus transport from Sousse and professional event photography (Aïcha Studio); 4) Competitive innovation awards, notably winning First Place and securing 600 TND in cash prize funding from tech sponsor TRAE.ai at the SPARK & SHIP Hackathon; and 5) Targeted alumni philanthropy, including an earmarked overseas donation from a former chapter president residing in Germany dedicated to boosting digital outreach.

Q8: What approach does your LOM adopt to engage its members in fundraising efforts and develop their capacity to contribute to resource mobilization?
Resource mobilization is structured as an integral leadership development pathway for all members. Action directors and committee members participate in internal treasury workshops covering sponsorship pitch design, negotiation techniques, and budgeting fundamentals. By embedding young members into sponsor negotiation teams alongside experienced officers, members gain firsthand experience in corporate dialogue, contract structuring, and budget defense, transforming fundraising from an administrative task into an empowering leadership skill.

Q9: What financial results did your LOM achieve during the evaluation period? Present, where possible, the revenue trends, funds raised, breakdown of funding sources, and achievement of set objectives.
During the 2026 evaluation period, JCI Sahline maintained complete budgetary balance across all eight major community initiatives with zero fiscal deficit. Total mobilized value was characterized by a healthy ratio of cash revenues, competitive prize funds, and substantial in-kind contributions. Major mobilized results include: 600 TND in direct competitive cash prize revenue from TRAE.ai; comprehensive corporate sponsorship of delegate transport (two chartered buses) and event materials from AMAD Groupe; complete venue cost waivers at Technopole Monastir, Maison de la Culture, and Hoist Gym; and thousands of dinars in equivalent in-kind catering, media, and decoration services. This diversified mobilization enabled JCI Sahline to execute 100% of its planned annual action calendar without exhausting local reserve funds.

Q10: How have the implemented financial strategies contributed to strengthening your LOM's financial stability, funding its projects, and ensuring the continuity of its activities? What mechanisms will help maintain these results in the long term?
Our resource-matching model insulated the chapter against cash flow volatility. By funding event infrastructure through direct corporate partnerships rather than upfront liquid reserves, chapter funds remained preserved for strategic investments, leadership training, and emergency community actions. Continuity is institutionalized through two mechanisms: formal multi-year partner relationships with educational and municipal institutions, and an archived treasury knowledge repository that transfers budgeting templates, sponsor registries, and supplier rate sheets from outgoing to incoming treasurers.

Q11 :What innovative approaches has your LOM developed regarding financial management, sponsorship, or fundraising, and how do they differ from standard practices?
Our innovation lies in operational resource-matching and competitive venture monetization. Standard non-profit practices rely heavily on soliciting cash donations. In contrast, JCI Sahline identified non-monetary operational bottlenecks—such as regional transport, professional audio-visual staging, and specialized venue space—and secured these as direct in-kind corporate contributions, eliminating cash intermediation. Furthermore, we pioneered competitive grant capture by deploying our members' technical skills to win regional innovation hackathons, converting project design directly into treasury capital.

Q12: What best practices, tools, or lessons learned from your experience could be replicated by other JCI LOMs?
Other JCI chapters can replicate three core lessons: 1) Conduct an operational needs assessment before approaching sponsors, asking partners for specific resources they can easily supply (spaces, buses, catering) rather than generic cash; 2) Maintain a standardized in-kind valuation ledger to capture the full economic footprint of community projects; and 3) Establish an internal treasury mentorship framework where former treasurers actively coach incoming officers, preserving financial governance standards across leadership transitions.
