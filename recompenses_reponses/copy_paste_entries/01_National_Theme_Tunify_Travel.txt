Project purpose (100-word limit):
Tunify Travel is a digital tourism concept developed by JCI Sahline to help visitors discover Tunisian destinations and plan trips using organized destination information, an interactive map, itinerary planning, and personalized recommendations. It responds to fragmented tourism information and limited visibility for lesser-known attractions. The project was presented at the SPARK & SHIP tourism hackathon on 5 April 2026, where the team won first place and a reported 600 TND prize for JCI Sahline. The platform’s commercial deployment and tourism impact remain under development and are not presented as completed outcomes.

Project overview (150-word limit):
TouristUp developed and presented Tunify Travel, a proposed digital platform for discovering destinations, activities, accommodation, and services in Tunisia. The concept combines an interactive geospatial map, trip-planning tools, personalized recommendations, and a proposed commission-based model connecting travelers with tourism businesses. JCI Sahline’s team presented the concept at the SPARK & SHIP tourism hackathon on 5 April 2026 and received first place; the report states that the 600 TND prize was paid to the organization. The entry supports the national tourism theme through discovery and business innovation.
