Working copy based on the 2026 award forms and the reports currently in rapport_texts/.

HOW TO USE
- Each numbered file is a separate award/category draft. Copy the answer text into the corresponding portal fields only after checking the internal notes at the end.
- This folder is the single source of truth for award-entry drafts. Do not create parallel copies at the recompenses_reponses/ root.
- Original action reports remain in rapport_texts/. The optional consolidated copy of all reports is recompenses_reponses/rapports_consolides.txt.
- Bracketed fields such as [VERIFY ...] are not facts and must be replaced or removed before submission.
- No invented attendance, satisfaction, budget, vaccination, member-growth, media-reach, or impact statistics are included. Where the source report has a number, it is identified as reported and must be supported by evidence.
- Do not turn plans, estimates, targets, or animals received into completed outcomes.
- The 2025 criteria file says one project may be entered in only one category, entries must be in English, and photo/video proofs are mandatory. Confirm all rules with the 2026 organizer. These drafts assign each action to one category only.
- Check every word limit in the current portal. Some prompts below are concise field labels; the portal's official question remains controlling.
